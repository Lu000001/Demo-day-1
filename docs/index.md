# MESSAGEix-China-Demo

Explore how a small energy-system model works: install it, follow the energy and material flows, change assumptions, and compare the results.

The model represents China as one region in 2030 and 2035. It covers electricity, heat, steel and fixed energy demand in buildings, transport and other industry. All inputs are included in the repository.
